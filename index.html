Ph.D. Student · Materials Science & Engineering · Stanford University 博士生 · 材料科學與工程學系 · 史丹佛大學

I am a Ph.D. student in Materials Science and Engineering at Stanford University. I want to understand how epitaxial structure, defect chemistry, and interfacial energetics govern charge transport in complex oxides. Using epitaxial BiVO4 thin films and heterostructures as model systems, I combine solution-based epitaxy with synchrotron X-ray probes to connect crystal symmetry, strain, and oxygen vacancies to carrier dynamics and (photo)electrocatalytic performance. Before Stanford, I earned my B.S. and M.S. in Chemistry at National Taiwan University, where I was the first undergraduate researcher in Prof. Chang-Ming Jiang's group. 我是史丹佛大學材料科學與工程學系博士生。 我的研究興趣是磊晶結構、缺陷化學與介面能量如何主導複雜氧化物中的電荷傳輸。 我以磊晶 BiVO4 薄膜與異質結構為模型系統，結合溶液法磊晶製程與同步輻射 X 光技術， 探討晶體對稱性、應變與氧空缺如何影響載子動力學與（光）電催化表現。 此前我在國立臺灣大學化學系取得學士與碩士學位，也是 Chang-Ming Jiang 教授研究室的第一位大學部專題生。

Research研究方向

Solution-based oxide epitaxy溶液法氧化物磊晶

Developed a solution-based route to epitaxial BiVO4 and established its rapid-thermal-annealing conversion mechanism, validated by XRD and synchrotron measurements. (010) films show ~50% higher photocatalytic activity than (001) films. 建立溶液法磊晶 BiVO4 的製程，並以 XRD 與同步輻射量測解析其快速熱退火轉化機制；發現 (010) 取向薄膜的光催化活性比 (001) 高約 50%。

Interface-engineered heterostructures介面工程異質結構

Engineered BiVO4/ITO/YSZ heterostructures with coherent interfaces; tuning thermal budget and annealing atmosphere gives reliable orientation control and reveals pronounced facet-dependent anisotropy in charge transfer. 設計具有同調介面的 BiVO4/ITO/YSZ 異質結構，透過調控熱預算與退火氣氛達成穩定的取向控制，揭示電荷轉移中顯著的晶面異向性。

Synchrotron X-ray probes同步輻射 X 光探測

Independently designed experiments at NSRRC combining reciprocal space mapping, pump–probe resonant scattering, and XAS to track twin-domain evolution, carrier-trapping pathways, VO4 distortions, and oxidation-state changes during operation. 於國家同步輻射研究中心獨立設計實驗，結合倒空間映射（RSM）、泵浦–探測共振散射與 XAS，追蹤孿晶域演變、載子捕獲路徑、VO4 結構扭曲與操作過程中的氧化態變化。

Looking ahead, I aim to build oxide heterostructure platforms that reveal the fundamental links among structure, defects, and transport — using operando characterization to follow defect evolution and interfacial charge transfer in working materials. 未來我希望建立氧化物異質結構平台，釐清結構、缺陷與傳輸之間的根本關聯，並透過臨場（operando）表徵追蹤材料運作中的缺陷演變與介面電荷轉移。
